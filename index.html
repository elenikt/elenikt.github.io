Eleni Koutsiona

M.Sc. Astrophysics @ Heidelberg University · ESA Research Trainee

I am a master's student in Astrophysics at Heidelberg University (Astronomisches Rechen-Institut / ZAH) working with Prof. Dr. Dominika Wylezalek on gas, dust, and feedback in high-redshift radio galaxies. Currently, I am also a trainee at the European Space Agency (ESTEC) conducting reverberation mapping simulations of Active Galactic Nuclei.
